Install SOLID 20

Custom firmware can fail and leave your FM-1 in update mode. Have a verified recovery method ready. Do not disconnect USB while writing. Use at your own risk.

Chrome or Edge on desktop is required for WebMIDI SysEx. Connect the FM-1 directly by a USB data cable, and close other MIDI apps.

The firmware is loaded from this project's GitHub Pages site. It is checked locally against the exact published SHA-256 before installation.
